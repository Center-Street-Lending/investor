Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Who we are

Center Street Capital (“Center Street Capital,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates www.centerstreetcapital.com (the “Site”). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share personal information in connection with the Site and related professional communications. It should be read with our Cookie Policy.

Scope

This Policy applies to personal information collected through the Site, contact forms, cookies, general inquiries to hello@centerstreetcapital.com, and diligence-access requests to ir@centerstreetcapital.com.

Certain information we collect from individual investors after they subscribe to a Fund may be “nonpublic personal information” subject to the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and Regulation S-P. That information, if applicable, is described in a separate U.S. Consumer Privacy Notice and is generally outside the California Consumer Privacy Act to the extent that statute’s GLBA exemption applies. Information about website visitors and people who have not entered a customer relationship is covered by this Policy.

Information we collect

You provide: name, firm, title, email address, phone number, and the content of messages you send us, including diligence-access requests. Do not submit Social Security numbers, government IDs, or wire instructions through the public Site.

Collected automatically: IP address, device and browser type, referring URL, pages viewed, dates and times of visits, and cookie identifiers, as described in the Cookie Policy.

From others: publicly available professional information and information from service providers who host or analyze the Site.

How we use information

We use personal information to: operate, secure, and improve the Site; respond to general inquiries and diligence-access requests and keep professional records of those communications; protect against fraud, abuse, and security incidents; comply with law and respond to lawful requests; and, if you later enter a subscription process, carry out eligibility review and required onboarding through controlled, non-public channels — not through this public Site.

How we share information

We share personal information with service providers who host the Site, provide email or form processing, and (if enabled) analytics, under contracts that limit their use of the data. We may share information with professional advisers as needed to operate the firm, and when required by law or to protect rights and safety. We may transfer information in connection with a reorganization, merger, or sale of assets.

Sale and sharing. We do not sell personal information for money. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use advertising or retargeting pixels. If that changes, we will update this Policy and provide a “Do Not Sell or Share” link.

Retention and security

We retain inquiry and diligence-request records for as long as needed to respond and to meet recordkeeping and legal obligations, then delete or de-identify them when no longer needed. Website logs are typically retained for a shorter operational period. Investor records, once a subscription process begins, are retained as required by applicable books-and-records and anti-money-laundering rules. We use commercially reasonable safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

California Privacy Notice and Notice at Collection

This section applies to California residents and supplements the rest of this Policy. It is also our notice at collection. It does not apply to personal information that is subject to the GLBA / Regulation S-P exemption described above.

Categories we may collect: identifiers (name, email, phone, postal address, IP address, device ID); customer-records information (name, phone, employment, information you include in a message); commercial information (records of materials requested); internet activity (pages viewed, referring URL, cookie IDs); and professional information (title, firm). Sources are you, your browser, public professional sources, and service providers. We use these categories to respond to inquiries and diligence requests, operate and secure the Site, and route communications. We do not sell or share these categories. We do not seek Social Security numbers or government IDs via the public Site.

Your California rights (subject to verification and legal exceptions): know/access; delete; correct; opt out of sale or sharing; limit use and disclosure of sensitive personal information; data portability; and non-discrimination.

How to submit a request: email hello@centerstreetcapital.com with the subject line “California Privacy Request,” or write to the address below. We will verify the request (typically by matching the email address and name we already have). You may use an authorized agent. We will acknowledge requests within 10 business days and respond within 45 days (or 90 days if reasonably extended, with notice).

Appeals: if we deny a request, reply to our decision with “Privacy Appeal” in the subject line. Where required, we treat a valid Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out of sale/sharing for that browser. We do not use sensitive personal information collected from the public Site to infer characteristics or for advertising.

Other U.S. state rights

Residents of certain other states may have similar rights to access, correct, delete, or opt out of targeted advertising or sales. Use the same contact method. We will process requests as required by applicable law.

Changes

We may update this Policy by posting a new version with a revised date. Material changes to our collection practices will be reflected in an updated notice at collection.
