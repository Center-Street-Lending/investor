Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Center Street Capital uses cookies and similar technologies on www.centerstreetcapital.com. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device. They may be first-party (set by us) or third-party (set by a vendor). Session cookies expire when you close the browser. Persistent cookies last until they expire or you delete them. We also use similar technologies such as pixels and local storage.

Cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies: security, load balancing, storing your cookie preferences, preventing abuse. The Site may not function without these. No additional consent required.

Analytics cookies (optional): understand aggregate traffic and which pages are used, so we can maintain the Site. These are non-essential and should remain off until accepted.

Advertising / retargeting cookies: not used on this introduction site.

Your choices

You may manage non-essential cookies through the Cookie Settings link in the footer, through your browser settings, and through recognized opt-out signals such as the Global Privacy Control. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect Site functionality. California residents may opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information as described in the Privacy Policy.
