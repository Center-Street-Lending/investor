// OVERVIEW

Short-duration residential credit
with active oversight.

CSL RTL provides senior secured financing against residential transitional and value-add projects. Every loan is originated, underwritten, and managed by the Center Street team, with no outsourced servicing and no brokered pipeline.

Many residential transitional opportunities sit outside the focus of large institutional lenders and traditional private credit funds. CSL RTL was built to underwrite them with the same discipline applied to any institutional credit — and to stay on the position through maturity.

What we finance

  • –Residential transitional and value-add projects
  • –First-lien, asset-based structures sized to protect principal
  • –Shorter expected hold periods relative to traditional private credit
  • –Projects where active oversight and execution drive outcomes
// CAPITAL PRESERVATION

Principal protection comes
before yield.

The strategy prioritizes principal protection through conservative loan sizing, first-lien collateral, and active project oversight. Outcomes are driven by successful execution and asset-level value creation, not by leverage.
