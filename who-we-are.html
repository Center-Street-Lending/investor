// THE FIRM

Why Center Street Capital exists.

We underwrite the way owners think: through full cycles, downside first, with our own capital committed alongside investors. Origination, underwriting, servicing and reporting are performed by our own professionals.

Today we oversee two strategies, each run by its own investment team. Center Street Lending RTL, our residential transitional lending strategy, is managed by Center Street Capital. Riviera Capital Partners, which invests equity and credit in the lower middle market, is managed by Riviera Capital, where Stephen Couig serves as a general partner. The teams are entirely separate.

// PHILOSOPHY

What we believe.

01

Underwrite for the full cycle

We structure for the downside before we consider the upside, informed by careers spent lending and investing through multiple cycles.

02

Full alignment, not optics

Our capital sits beside our investors’. Underwriting is never delegated, and the team that commits capital answers for it over the life of the investment.

03

Preservation before yield

Conservative structure, real collateral and enforceable covenants come first. Yield is never a reason to compromise them.

// GOVERNANCE

Two committees. No shortcuts.

Center Street Lending RTL operates under two committees: one sets the standards, the other rules on every exception. Riviera Capital Partners is governed separately by its own investment committee.

Investment Committee

Sets the underwriting standards for Center Street Lending RTL and oversees portfolio monitoring, bringing together credit and operational perspectives.

Credit Committee

Draws on underwriting, capital markets, valuation and senior management to review any loan outside guidelines and to structure compensating protections where needed.

Full committee composition, approval workflows, and portfolio monitoring detail are available to accredited and qualified investors through diligence materials.
