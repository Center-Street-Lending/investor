// HOW WE INVEST

Different strategies. The same convictions.

SOURCING

Earned, not bought

Opportunities come from relationships built over years, not from purchased deal flow.

UNDERWRITING

Written criteria

Every investment is measured against written standards for structure, collateral and sponsor quality. Exceptions are escalated, never assumed.

OWNERSHIP

Accountable for the full life

The professionals who underwrite an investment answer for it until it is repaid or exited. Underwriting is never delegated.

ALIGNMENT

Our capital, beside yours

We invest our own capital alongside investors in every strategy.
