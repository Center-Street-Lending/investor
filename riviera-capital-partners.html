// THE RIVIERA DIFFERENCE

What sets the sourcing and structure apart.

Riviera was built around a different pipeline and a different definition of alignment — one that feeds both credit and equity opportunities.

Deal Sourcing

A proprietary, off-market pipeline built through a network of 20+ MBA/JD searchers — senior operators who source and remain involved, not junior analysts screening from the sidelines.

Operating Continuity

In private equity, the searcher who sources the opportunity becomes the operating partner running the business day-to-day. In direct lending, the same network surfaces high-quality credit opportunities with real operator insight.

Alignment

GP capital is invested alongside every limited partner in every deal, backed by Center Street's underwriting infrastructure, credit discipline, and balance sheet.

// APPROACH

A disciplined path from sourcing to outcome.

Every opportunity moves through a repeatable process. The searcher who brings the deal stays accountable to the result.

01 — Source

Proprietary Pipeline

Built through a network of MBA/JD searchers — senior operators, not junior deal-screeners.

02 — Acquire

Underwrite & Close

Rigorous underwriting against defined criteria, with the incoming operator embedded from day one.

03 — Operate

Aligned Ownership

In equity, the searcher runs the business. In credit, operator insight informs structure and monitoring.

04 — Grow & Exit

Disciplined Outcome

Active portfolio support toward a disciplined exit, preserving what made the opportunity worth underwriting.
