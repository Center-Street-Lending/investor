// THE RIVIERA DIFFERENCE

A different pipeline. A deeper form of alignment.

Riviera begins where most capital doesn’t: with operators who find businesses directly and stay with them.

Deal Sourcing

Off-market opportunities found by senior operators who stay involved, not by analysts screening from a distance.

Operating Continuity

In private equity, the searcher who finds the business becomes the executive who runs it. In credit, the same network brings an operator’s eye to every loan.

Alignment

Riviera invests its own capital alongside limited partners in every transaction, and its own team underwrites and oversees each one.

// APPROACH

From first conversation to exit.

Every opportunity follows the same process, and the operator who brings it stays accountable for the result.

01 — Source

Proprietary Pipeline

Built through a network of MBA/JD searchers — senior operators, not junior deal-screeners.

02 — Acquire

Underwrite & Close

Rigorous underwriting against defined criteria, with the incoming operator embedded from day one.

03 — Operate

Aligned Ownership

In equity, the searcher runs the business. In credit, operator insight informs structure and monitoring.

04 — Grow & Exit

Disciplined Outcome

Active portfolio support toward a disciplined exit, preserving what made the opportunity worth underwriting.
