// THE RIVIERA DIFFERENCE

A different pipeline. A deeper form of alignment.

Riviera begins where most capital doesn’t: with operators who find businesses directly and stay with them.

Deal Sourcing

Every opportunity arrives through a searcher who intends to run the business, either a traditional search fund Riviera has backed or a self-funded searcher already under letter of intent. Nothing comes from a banker's process.

Operating Continuity

In private equity, the searcher who finds the business becomes the executive who runs it. In credit, the same network brings an operator's eye to every loan.

Alignment

Riviera invests its own capital alongside limited partners in every transaction. The three partners read every searcher and every proposed acquisition themselves; every decision is unanimous.

// APPROACH

From first conversation to exit.

Every opportunity follows the same process, and the operator who brings it stays accountable for the result.

01 — Meet operator first

Meet the operator first

Before the business, the person. Background, motivation and how someone handles being told no.

02 — Written criteria

Screen against written criteria

Recurring revenue, margin, growth history and customer concentration are tested before anyone builds a model.

03 — Underwrite & close

Underwrite the business, then close

Quality of earnings, customer calls, systems review and a first-hundred-days plan, with a board constructed before the wire goes out.

04 — Board & exit

Serve on board; exit on timeline

Monthly reporting, quarterly reviews, and a sale prepared years before it is run.

// EQUITY & CREDIT

Private equity first. Credit alongside it.

Riviera is a private equity firm with a credit strategy that extends the equity work — leveraging the same network and operator insight across both asset classes.

PRIVATE EQUITY

Operator-led acquisitions

Control and significant minority positions in owner-operated companies from $2 million of EBITDA to north of $10 million, acquired at 6x or less. The searcher who found the business runs it, with meaningful personal equity. Riviera takes the board seat and holds for as long as the company needs.

PRIVATE CREDIT

Direct corporate lending

Senior secured, cash-pay loans with full covenant packages to companies up to $10 million of EBITDA. Loans go to two kinds of borrower: Riviera's own portfolio companies, and businesses Riviera diligenced and liked but passed on for price or size.
