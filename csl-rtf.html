// OVERVIEW

An underserved market,
underwritten like institutional credit.

CSL RTL lends against residential projects that are too short-dated and too hands-on for bank capital. Each loan is sourced through long-standing relationships, underwritten by our own credit team and serviced without outside vendors.

The demand is structural. America’s housing stock is aging and undersupplied, sustaining a steady pipeline of renovation and new construction. We bring institutional underwriting to that pipeline and service every loan through payoff, whether it is held, securitized or sold.

What we finance

  • –Value-add resale, ground-up construction, bridge and rental loans to experienced residential investors
  • –Single-family and multifamily collateral
  • –First-lien, asset-based structures with conservative advance limits
  • –Short-term loans with defined maturities
  • –Projects where hands-on oversight protects the outcome
// CAPITAL PRESERVATION

We underwrite the downside first.

Every loan is built on layered safeguards: a first-lien position, conservative advance limits, borrower cash equity at closing, a full personal guarantee, and a sponsor with verified credit and completed projects. Short terms limit exposure to any one point in the cycle. When a project stalls, our own team steps in to finish and resolve it.

These features are designed to reduce risk, not eliminate it. Private credit investments can lose value.

// OUR APPROACH

One team, from first call
to final payoff.

No handoffs. No outsourced servicing. No gaps in accountability.

1
SOURCING

Relationships, not lead lists

A nationwide network of residential real estate professionals and repeat borrowers, each opportunity evaluated directly by the investment team.

2
UNDERWRITING

Written criteria, every loan

Advance limits, borrower cash equity, a full personal guarantee, a verified track record and a credit screen. Loans outside guidelines go to the Credit Committee.

// STRATEGY PROFILE

Why the structure matters.

Short, defined terms

Loans repay as projects are completed and sold or refinanced, rather than over multi-year holds.

Hard assets, personal recourse

A first lien on residential real estate, backed by borrower equity and a full personal guarantee.

Independent of corporate credit

Repayment comes from residential property, not operating-company cash flows.
